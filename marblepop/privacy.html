Privacy Policy

Marble Pop (com.marblepop.ballsort.game) · Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Marble Pop (the "Game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is made by fluffy Ai ("we", "us"). If you have any question, email appsmagic2942@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, settings) is stored on your device.
  • The Game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your device's advertising ID.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand how the Game is played, find crashes and tune difficulty.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.
  • You can delete your data at any time: in the Game (Settings → Delete account & data) or by email.

1. Information collected

  • Game progress and settings (levels, stars, coins, boosters, sound and accessibility settings). Why: To save your game. By: Stored on your device.
  • Advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate country or region), device model, OS version, ad interactions. Why: To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. By: Google AdMob and its ad partners.
  • App interactions (for example levels started and completed, boosters and ads used), app version, device type, a random app-instance ID. Why: Analytics: to fix difficulty, improve features and understand which features players enjoy. By: Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics (stack traces, device state at the time of a crash). Why: To find and fix bugs. By: Google Firebase Crashlytics.
  • A random anonymous player ID and a copy of your game progress. Why: Optional cloud save, so progress can be restored. By: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore).
  • Name and email from your Google account, only if you choose to sign in with Google. Why: To link your cloud save to your account. By: Google Firebase Authentication.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, audio or health information. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device and are off unless you turn them on.

2. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. It may also show banner and full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use cookies, your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to show and measure ads. Learn more: How Google uses information in ads (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) appears before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • iOS: the Game asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency before using your advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalized using that identifier.

3. In-app purchases

The Game currently has no paid items. If paid items are added, payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We would receive a transaction record (product, time, order ID) to deliver the item, but never your card or payment details.

4. Service providers

We share data only with these providers, which process it on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Authentication, Firestore) — Firebase privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of players and of us.

5. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it in the Game or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud saves stay until you delete your data in the Game or ask us to delete them.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Crashlytics reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Ad providers keep data according to their own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: in the Game, open Settings → Delete account & data. This removes your progress on the device and, if you used cloud save, your cloud save and anonymous account. You can also follow the steps on our data deletion page (https://mdgoaltech-web.github.io/marblepop/delete-account.html).
  • Ads: change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available) or in your device's ad settings.
  • Notifications: turn reminders on or off in Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or US states such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email appsmagic2942@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Our legal bases (EEA/UK) are: performing our agreement with you to provide the Game; our legitimate interests in keeping the Game secure, working and improving it; and your consent for personalized ads where required.

7. Children

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated PG or below. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security and transfers

Data sent from the Game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the Game.
