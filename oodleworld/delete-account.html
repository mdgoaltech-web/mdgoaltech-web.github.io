Delete your data

Oodle World: Kids Play & Learn (com.fluffyai.oodleworld) · Effective November 11, 2026

Oodle World has no accounts and keeps everything on your device, so there is nothing stored on our servers to delete.

Erase progress on the device

  • Open the app, tap the shield (Parent zone), answer the grown-up question.
  • Tap Erase all progress on this device and confirm. Stars, stickers, hunts, the nickname and settings are deleted at once.
  • Deleting the app from the device removes the same data.

Purchases

Oodle Plus is managed by Apple or Google. Restore it on a new device with Restore purchases; cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Crash reports and analytics

If a build includes Firebase, anonymous crash reports and usage events may exist for up to 60 days. To ask for their deletion, email appsmagic2942@gmail.com with the subject "Oodle World data deletion".
