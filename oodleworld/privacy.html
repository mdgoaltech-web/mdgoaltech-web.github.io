Privacy Policy

Oodle World: Kids Play & Learn (com.fluffyai.oodleworld) · Effective November 11, 2026

Oodle World is made by Fluffy AI for children aged 3 and up and the grown-ups who care for them. We designed it to collect as little as possible.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not create accounts and never ask for an email, phone number or password.
  • We do not show advertising of any kind, and we have no ad networks in the app.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any data.
  • We do not use third-party tracking or behavioral advertising SDKs.

Magic Hunt and the camera

Magic Hunt lets a child show Oodle a real object using the camera or a photo from the library. The picture is analyzed entirely on the device by on-device image recognition (Google ML Kit, bundled model, no server call). The picture is deleted immediately after it is checked. It is never saved in the app, never uploaded and never shared. Camera and photo access are optional; the app works without them.

Doodle Guess

Finger drawings are recognized on the device. The recognizer needs a small language model that is downloaded once from Google when the activity is first opened; your drawings are never sent anywhere.

Data stored on the device

Stars, stickers, hunt progress, settings, a daily play-time counter and an optional first name or nickname a grown-up may type in the Parent zone. All of this stays in the app's local storage on your device and can be erased at any time from Parent zone > Erase all progress. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Purchases

Oodle Plus is sold through Apple App Store or Google Play. The store handles payment and billing; we never see your card details. Purchases are protected by a parent gate.

Analytics and crash reports

The app may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which activities are used and to fix crashes. These reports contain no names, photos, drawings or contact details, and they are not used for advertising. If analytics is not configured in a build, nothing is sent.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not contain social features, chat, external links reachable by a child, or user-generated content shared with others. Links and purchases sit behind a grown-up gate.

Your choices

  • Camera and photos: deny or revoke the permission in your device Settings.
  • Erase local data: Parent zone > Erase all progress.
  • Questions or deletion requests: appsmagic2942@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and the copy inside the app.
