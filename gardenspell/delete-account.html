Delete your Garden Spell data

Garden Spell (com.gardenspell.match3.merge.puzzle) by fluffy Ai

Garden Spell has no accounts. Your progress is saved only on your device.

In the Game (instant)

  1. Open Garden Spell.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Under Support & legal, tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

This permanently erases your progress, coins, items and settings on the device. Uninstalling the Game also removes them.

By email

For any other request, email appsmagic2942@gmail.com with the subject Garden Spell data request. We reply within 30 days.

Advertising data

Google AdMob keeps ad data under its own policies. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
