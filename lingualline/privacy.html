Lingual Line Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Lingual Line is a language-learning app published by Fluffy AI ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Lingual Line has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers.
  • Lingual Line contains no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device.
  • The Translator works on your device. The component that powers it, made by Google, sends Google technical usage and diagnostic information, described below. The text you translate is not sent.

Information stored on your device

Lingual Line saves the following on your device only, so the app can work:

  • The name you choose to enter (optional).
  • The language you speak, the language you are learning, your daily goal and your settings.
  • Your learning progress: lessons, unit tests and quizzes completed, quiz scores, words practised and when they are due for review, XP, streak, daily goals and milestones.
  • How many translations you have made today.

This information never leaves your device through Lingual Line. If you delete the app, or use "Erase all progress" in the app, it is removed. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data according to your device settings; that is controlled by you and by Apple or Google.

Spoken words

Lingual Line reads words and sentences aloud using the text-to-speech voices built into your device. The text is passed to your device's operating system. Lingual Line does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Translator

The Translator turns a sentence you type into another language and can read it aloud. It uses Google's ML Kit on-device translation.

  • The text you type and its translation stay on your device. They are not sent to us or to Google.
  • The first time you translate between two languages, the app downloads a language pack of about 30 MB per language from Google. After that, translation works offline.
  • ML Kit sends Google technical information about how the component is used, for diagnostics and usage analytics. According to Google, this includes: device information (such as manufacturer, model and operating system version), the app's identifier and version, a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you or your device, performance figures, the languages selected, the kind of event (such as a download or a translation) and error codes. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties.
  • This information goes to Google, not to us. We cannot see it. Google's own description is at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/ios-data-disclosure, and Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, Lingual Line schedules a local notification on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

Purchases

Lingual Line Plus, including any free trial, is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase is active, and the app checks this with the store when it opens.

Children

Lingual Line is suitable for a general audience. We do not collect personal data from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The technical information that Google's translation component sends is described above.

Your choices

  • You can change or remove your name at any time in the app.
  • You can erase all app data on your device with "Erase all progress".
  • You can switch reminders, sound and spoken words on or off in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version in the app. If a future version of Lingual Line starts to collect any data, we will ask first and update this policy before it does.
