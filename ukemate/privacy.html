UkeMate Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Publisher: Fluffy AI · Contact: appsmagic2942@gmail.com

UkeMate ("the app") teaches the ukulele. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app has no accounts and no advertising. Your progress and your songs stay on your device, and nothing the microphone hears ever leaves it. The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix problems and improve the lessons.

Microphone

The tuner, lessons, songs and Chord Finder listen to your ukulele through the microphone, but only while one of those screens is open and only if you allow it. The sound is analysed on your device in real time to find the pitch or chord. It is not recorded, not stored and not sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn microphone access off at any time in your device settings; the app still works, without listening.

Information kept on your device

Your language, answers to the welcome questions, settings, lesson and song progress, streak, practice time and the songs you create in Song Maker are saved on your device so the app can show them again. We cannot see this information. Deleting the app, or choosing Me → Delete my data, erases it.

Purchases

UkeMate Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store handles your payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The app asks the store whether a subscription is active on your store account and keeps that answer on your device. Apple and Google process purchase information under their own privacy policies.

Usage statistics and crash reports

The app uses two Google services, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive:

  • an anonymous app-instance identifier created by the app, which is reset when you delete the app;
  • general usage events, for example that a lesson or a song was finished and with how many stars, the language of the app, the answers to the welcome questions, that the subscription screen was shown, and whether a subscription is active;
  • technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, country or region derived from the internet address, and, when the app crashes, a crash report describing what the app was doing.

They do not receive your name or email address, anything from the microphone, or the titles or chords of songs you create. We use this information only to understand how the app is used and to fix faults. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell it. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy; analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

Your choices and rights

Deleting the app stops all collection and resets the app-instance identifier. Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask for access to, or deletion of, data linked to your app-instance identifier, or to object to its use. Email us and we will help; we may ask you for the identifier, because we have no other way to tell whose data is whose.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or photos. It contains no advertising, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask anyone for personal details. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete what we can.

Contacting us

If you email us for support we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
