Delete your Loop Jam data

Loop Jam (com.loopjam.cubesort.game) by fluffy Ai

In the Game (instant)

  1. Open Loop Jam.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to Support & legal and tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

This permanently deletes your game progress on the device and, if you used cloud save, your cloud save and anonymous player account.

By email (if you no longer have the Game)

Email appsmagic2942@gmail.com with the subject Loop Jam data deletion. If you signed in with Apple or Google, send it from that email address. We delete your cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: game progress, settings, cloud save, anonymous player ID and any linked sign-in.
  • Kept for a limited time: anonymous analytics (up to 14 months) and crash reports (up to 90 days), which are not linked to your name or email. Ad providers keep ad data under their own policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
