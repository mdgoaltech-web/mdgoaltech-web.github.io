Kuddly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Kuddly ("the app") is a self-care companion published by Afex Digital LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we do not collect personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your koala's name, your optional first name, your daily goals ("leaves"), water intake, mood check-ins, gratitude notes, breathing sessions, rewards, outfits and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We have no server that receives this data. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete all my data, erases it.

Camera

Two optional features use the camera: Mirror Moment (your koala mirrors your smile and suggests a mood) and Sip Scan (reads the volume printed on a bottle label). Both run entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit). Camera frames are processed in memory and are never stored, uploaded or shared. You can use every other feature without granting camera access.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers.

Purchases

Kuddly Plus is sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store processes payment; we never see your payment details. We store only a yes/no entitlement flag on the device. Use Restore purchases on a new device.

Analytics

If the app build includes Firebase Analytics, we receive anonymous usage events (for example "onboarding completed", "water logged") together with device type and country derived by Google. These events never contain your name, mood text, gratitude notes, camera images or any other personal content. We use them only to improve the app. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Kuddly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because data lives on your device, you control it directly. For questions or deletion requests about analytics data, contact support@afexdigital.com.

Changes

We will post any changes to this page and update the date above.
