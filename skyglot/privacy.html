Skyglot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Skyglot is published by Fluffy AI ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Skyglot app.

The short version

  • Skyglot has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers.
  • Skyglot contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking.
  • Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

Skyglot saves the following on your device only, so the app can work:

  • The name you choose to enter (optional).
  • Your chosen language, daily goal and settings.
  • Your learning progress: lessons finished, words practised, streak, light, stardust, quests and badges.

This information never leaves your device through Skyglot. If you delete the app, or use "Erase all progress" in the app, it is removed. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data according to your device settings; that is controlled by you and by Apple or Google.

Spoken words

Skyglot reads words aloud using the text-to-speech voices built into your device. The text is passed to your device's operating system. Skyglot does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, Skyglot schedules a local notification on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

Purchases

Skyglot Plus is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Children

Skyglot is suitable for a general audience. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • You can change or remove your name at any time in the app.
  • You can erase all app data on your device with "Erase all progress".
  • You can switch reminders, sound and spoken words on or off in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version in the app. If a future version of Skyglot starts to collect any data, we will ask first and update this policy before it does.
