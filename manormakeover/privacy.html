Privacy Policy: Manor Makeover: Match & Design

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Afex Digital ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Manor Makeover: Match & Design (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Contact: support@afexdigital.com

1. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account or entering any personal details.
  • The Game does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Some information about your device is used to show and measure those ads.
  • The Game has no in-app purchases.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device and backed up to Google Firebase under an anonymous ID so it can be restored. You may optionally link a Google account (or Apple on iPhone) to carry progress between devices.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played and Firebase Crashlytics to fix crashes.

2. Information we collect

  • Game progress and settings (levels, stars, coins, story choices, sound and notification preferences). Stored on your device so you can continue playing.
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version, approximate country derived from your IP address). Used by our ad and analytics providers to show ads and to understand how the Game performs.
  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking; on Android unless you reset or delete it) and app-instance identifiers. Used to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud.
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked or watched for a reward). Used to show and measure ads and to give you the reward you chose.
  • Gameplay events (for example level started or completed, story choices, ads and rewards used) and an app-instance ID, collected by Google Firebase Analytics. Used to understand how the Game is played, fix difficulty and improve it.
  • Crash reports (stack trace, device model, operating system and app version, current level) and a Crashlytics installation ID, collected by Google Firebase Crashlytics. Used to find and fix crashes.
  • A random anonymous player ID and a backup copy of your game progress, stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Used to back up and restore your progress.
  • If you choose to link an account: your Google account (or Apple ID) user ID, email address and name as shared by that provider. Used only to restore your progress on another device.
  • Remote configuration requests (app-instance ID) to Google Firebase Remote Config. Used to tune game settings such as ad frequency without an update.
  • Emails you send to support. Used only to answer you.

We do not collect payment details, precise location, contacts, photos, audio or health data.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Game, save your progress and back it up to the cloud.
  • To understand how the Game is used (Firebase Analytics); this is not used to show you ads.
  • To show ads, including rewarded videos you choose to watch, and to measure them.
  • To keep the Game stable and fix problems.
  • To answer support requests.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. On iOS we ask for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get every feature of the Game and may see non-personalised ads.

4. Who receives information

  • Google AdMob, to show and measure ads. Where you consent or where the law permits, Google may use identifiers for personalised ads under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Firebase, our service provider for analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, sign-in and cloud backup: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Notifications

If you turn them on, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example "your lives are full"), at most one per day. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your device until you delete it. Your cloud backup and anonymous account are kept until you delete them. You can erase everything at any time in Settings, then Delete account & data: this removes your progress on the device, your cloud backup and your Firebase account. Uninstalling the Game removes only the copy on the device; to delete the cloud backup after uninstalling, email support@afexdigital.com and we will delete it within 30 days (see https://mdgoaltech-web.github.io/manormakeover/delete-account.html). Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and Crashlytics reports for up to 90 days. Data held by Google AdMob is kept under Google's retention policies.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ad privacy choices: in Settings (shown where required).
  • Tracking (iOS): Settings app, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android): Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • Notifications: in the Game's Settings or your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your information. Email support@afexdigital.com and we will help.

8. Children

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support@afexdigital.com and we will delete it.

9. Security

Information sent to our providers is encrypted in transit. Your cloud backup can be read and written only by your own account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above. For important changes we will also tell you in the Game.
