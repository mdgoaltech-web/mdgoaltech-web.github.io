Privacy Policy

Blendy: Learn to Read · Publisher: Fluffy AI · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Blendy is a reading app a grown-up and a child use together. It is built so that it does not need to know who you are.

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no adverts.
  • Your child's name, age and progress stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • Photos used by Word Lens are read on the device and deleted. They are never uploaded.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, so we can fix problems and see which features help.

What stays on your device

  • The first name (or nickname) and age you type for each child, and their lesson progress.
  • Your settings, such as the app language.

This is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet and is never sent to us. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), if you have it switched on, may include it; that backup is controlled by you and by Apple or Google. Deleting the app, or choosing Parents → Erase everything, removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

Blendy uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records anonymous events such as "a lesson was finished", "the offer was shown" or "Word Lens was used", together with general device information (model, operating system version, app version, language, country or region) and a random app-instance identifier that is reset when the app is reinstalled.
  • Firebase Crashlytics records technical details when the app stops working (the error, device model, operating system version and a random installation identifier), so the fault can be found and fixed.

These reports never contain a child's name or age, anything you type, the words read, photos, or the text found in photos. We do not use the advertising identifier, we do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with anyone other than Google acting as our service provider. Google's handling of this data is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

The legal basis for this processing (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies) is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

Camera and photos (Word Lens)

Word Lens asks to use the camera or a photo you choose, so it can find words on a page or a sign. The photo is read on the device by Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs offline inside the app. The photo is not uploaded and is deleted as soon as it has been read. ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic and performance information about the software component itself (for example, whether it ran correctly); it does not include your photos or the recognised text. See ML Kit terms.

Voice

Words are read aloud by the speech voice built into your device. Blendy does not record or listen to anything.

Purchases

Blendy Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card or account details. The app only learns from the store whether a subscription is active.

Children

Blendy is set up and used by a parent or carer together with a child. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the anonymous reports described above carry no name, age, contact detail or advertising identifier. Settings, child profiles and purchases started inside the app are behind a question for grown-ups.

Deleting your data

Names and progress exist only on your device: open Parents → Erase everything or delete the app to remove them. The anonymous reports cannot be linked to you by us, but if you would like help with a request about them, write to the address below and include the date you installed the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data, or to object to its use. Write to us at the address below and we will answer within 30 days.
