Privacy Policy: SewMate - Learn Sewing

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Afex Digital ("we", "us") makes the mobile app SewMate - Learn Sewing (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and the choices you have.

Contact: support@afexdigital.com

1. Summary

  • We do not collect any personal information. You can use the App without creating an account.
  • The App has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your answers to the welcome quiz (sewing experience, machine, favourite materials and styles, goals, weekly time and an optional first name). Used only to build your sewing plan and to show your name on certificates.
  • Your lesson progress, saved projects and certificates.
  • Body measurements you type into the Measurement Book or the Pattern Maker. Used only to draft your patterns.

This information is stored locally on your phone or tablet. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not backed up to our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Network use

  • On first launch the App downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. These requests contain no personal information. Google may receive your IP address as part of the normal internet connection; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Links to this website, to email support or to app stores open in your browser, email or store app.
  • If you choose to rate the App, the rating prompt is provided by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies.

4. Printing and sharing patterns

When you print or save a pattern PDF, the file is created on your device and handed to the printer or app you choose. We do not receive a copy.

5. Children

The App is intended for teenagers and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your choices

  • You can change or clear your measurements at any time in the App.
  • You can redo the welcome quiz from the Me tab.
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the App. See Delete your data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes (for example when new features are added), we will update this page and the date above.

8. Contact

Afex Digital – support@afexdigital.com
