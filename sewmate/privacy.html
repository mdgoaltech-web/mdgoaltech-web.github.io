Privacy Policy: SewMate - Learn Sewing

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Afex Digital ("we", "us") makes the mobile app SewMate - Learn Sewing (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and the choices you have.

Contact: support@afexdigital.com

1. Summary

  • You can use the App without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your quiz answers, progress and measurements stay on your device.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Subscribers see no ads.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes.
  • Subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your answers to the welcome quiz (sewing experience, machine, favourite materials and styles, goals, weekly time and an optional first name). Used only to build your sewing plan and to show your name on certificates.
  • Your lesson progress, watched videos, saved projects and certificates.
  • Body measurements you type into the Measurement Book or the Pattern Maker. Used only to draft your patterns.

This information is stored locally on your phone or tablet. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not backed up to our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes it. The App also remembers your chosen language and whether your subscription is active.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not subscribe, the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App under Me > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC). They collect an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, language, country (from IP address), which screens are used and crash details. We use this only to improve the App and fix problems; it is not used to identify you. See Firebase privacy and security.

5. Subscriptions

SewMate Premium is a weekly subscription with a free trial for eligible new subscribers, sold by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store handles payment and sends the App only a confirmation that your subscription is active. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Google Play or App Store account.

6. Other network use

  • Lesson videos are downloaded from our video host, GitHub Pages (run by GitHub, Inc.), the first time you play each one. The App then keeps a copy on your device, so later plays work offline. These requests contain no personal information. GitHub may receive your IP address as part of the normal internet connection; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • On first launch the App downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. These requests contain no personal information. Google may receive your IP address as part of the normal internet connection; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Links to this website, to email support or to app stores open in your browser, email or store app.
  • If you choose to rate the App, the rating prompt is provided by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies.

7. Printing and sharing patterns

When you print or save a pattern PDF, the file is created on your device and handed to the printer or app you choose. We do not receive a copy.

8. Children

The App is intended for teenagers and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Ads in the App are not directed at children.

9. Your choices

  • Subscribe to remove all ads, or change ad consent under Me > Ad privacy choices.
  • Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email us with the date you installed the App.
  • You can change or clear your measurements at any time in the App.
  • You can redo the welcome quiz from the Me screen (the person icon on the Home screen).
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the App. See Delete your data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes (for example when new features are added), we will update this page and the date above.
