Delete your data: SewMate - Learn Sewing

SewMate does not have accounts and does not store any of your data on our servers. Your quiz answers, progress, saved projects and measurements exist only on your device.

How to delete everything

  • Android: Settings › Apps › SewMate › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the App.
  • iPhone / iPad: uninstall the App (touch and hold the icon › Remove App › Delete App).

To clear only your measurements, open the App › Tools › Measurements and delete the values.
