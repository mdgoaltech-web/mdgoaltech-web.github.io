Delete your data: SewMate - Learn Sewing

SewMate (by Afex Digital) has no accounts. Your quiz answers, progress, saved projects and measurements exist only on your device. Analytics and crash data (Google Firebase) and ad data (Google AdMob) are linked to a random app or advertising ID, never to your name or email.

1. Delete the data on your device

  • Android: Settings › Apps › SewMate › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the App.
  • iPhone / iPad: uninstall the App (touch and hold the icon › Remove App › Delete App).

To clear only your measurements, open the App › Tools › Measurements and delete the values.

2. Ask us to delete analytics and crash data

Email support@afexdigital.com with the subject "SewMate data deletion" and the approximate date you installed the App. We delete the Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data we can link to your request within 30 days and confirm by email. Without a request, Firebase deletes analytics data automatically after its retention period (no longer than 14 months) and crash reports after 90 days.

3. Advertising data

Ad data is held by Google. Reset or delete your advertising ID on your device (Android: Settings › Google › Ads / Privacy › Ads), change your choice in the App under Me › Ad privacy choices, or manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

What is kept

We keep nothing else. Subscription records are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies; cancelling or deleting the App does not delete your purchase history with them.
