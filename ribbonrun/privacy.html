Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Ribbon Run is a puzzle game made by Fluffy AI. It runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

What we collect

  • We do not collect personal information. Ribbon Run has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising.
  • Your progress, settings and a small local play log are saved only on your device. They never leave it.
  • Ribbon Run does not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and does not share data with anyone.

Purchases

  • On Google Play every chapter is free and there is nothing to buy.
  • On the Apple App Store the Full Collection is an optional one-time purchase. Apple processes it and we never see your payment details. The App Store only tells the game whether the Full Collection is owned, so it can unlock and restore it.

Children

  • Ribbon Run does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Ribbon Run removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
