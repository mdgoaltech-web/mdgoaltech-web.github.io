What Vendra is

Vendra helps you record orders, stock, payments and deliveries. It is not accounting, tax or legal software, does not verify payments, and is not connected to couriers, marketplaces or messaging apps. Reports are estimates based on what you enter. You are responsible for checking your records and keeping your own backups (use CSV export).

Vendra Pro subscription

  • Vendra Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription in your store account settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • If Pro ends, your data stays. Viewing, editing, exporting and recording payments always remain available on the free plan.
