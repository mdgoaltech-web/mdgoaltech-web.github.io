Vendra privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This policy covers the Vendra: Orders & Store Manager app for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Afex Digital LLC ("we").

The short version

Everything you enter in Vendra stays on your device. Vendra has no account, uploads nothing to us, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores on your device

The business details, customers, orders, products, stock movements, payments, expenses, tasks and files that you enter or attach. This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

Customer information you record

If you record your customers' names, phone numbers or addresses, you are responsible for having their permission and for keeping it accurate. Vendra only opens WhatsApp, SMS, phone or email when you tap a button, and you send every message yourself.

Device permissions

Photos and camera are used only when you choose to attach a picture to an order, product, expense or receipt. Notifications are used only for reminders you turn on. Vendra does not read your contacts, messages, location or microphone.

Purchases

Vendra Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app asks the store whether your subscription is active.

Exports and sharing

When you export CSV files or share a PDF, the file goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Use More → Data, export & privacy → Delete this business to erase a business from your device, or delete the app to remove everything. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Vendra is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If a future version adds optional cloud backup or team accounts, we will update this policy and ask for your consent in the app before anything is uploaded.
