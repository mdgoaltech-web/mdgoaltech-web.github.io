Privacy Policy

Piano Beats (com.afexdigital.pianobeats) by fluffy Ai

Last updated: 30 October 2026

fluffy Ai ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Piano Beats (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game uses, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

Contact: fluffy Ai · appsmagic2942@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. Your progress is saved on your device and, in the background, backed up to Google Firebase under an anonymous ID so it can be restored. You may optionally link a Google account (or Apple on iPhone) to carry progress between devices.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played and Firebase Crashlytics to fix crashes.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location permission.
  • The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, a banner on some menu screens, and occasional full-screen ads between songs or when you return to the Game. Ads never appear during a song.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads; on iOS we ask for permission before using your device's advertising identifier.
  • You can erase all Game data at any time in Settings → Delete account & data.

3. How information is used

  • Provide the Game — progress, rewards and settings are stored on your device and backed up to Firebase so they can be restored.
  • Analytics and stability — Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us understand gameplay and fix crashes. They are not used to show you ads.
  • Advertising — Google AdMob uses the information above to show ads (rewarded, banner, interstitial and app-open) and measure them. Personalized ads are used only with your consent where the law requires it; otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Notifications (optional, local on your device, at most one per day) — only if you allow them; turn them off any time in device settings.

4. Who receives information

  • Google (AdMob) — to show and measure ads under Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google (Firebase) — as our service provider for analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, sign-in and cloud backup: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Authorities — only if required by law.

We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Your cloud backup and anonymous account are kept until you delete them in the Game or ask us to. Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and Crashlytics crash reports for up to 90 days. Data held by Google for advertising is kept under Google's retention policies. Support emails are kept up to 24 months.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete account & data erases all Game data on your device and deletes your cloud backup and account from Firebase. Uninstalling the Game removes local data only; to delete the cloud backup after uninstalling, email us.
  • Ads consent: Settings → Ad privacy choices (where offered).
  • Advertising ID (Android): Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete or reset advertising ID.
  • Tracking (iOS): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Other rights (access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal of consent, opt-out of targeted advertising): email appsmagic2942@gmail.com.

7. Children

The Game is a general-audience music rhythm game for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information sent by the ads and Firebase SDKs is encrypted in transit. Your cloud backup can be read and written only by your own account.
