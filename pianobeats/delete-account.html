Delete your Piano Beats data

Piano Beats (com.afexdigital.pianobeats) by fluffy Ai

You do not need an account to play Piano Beats. Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to Google Firebase under an anonymous ID (or under the Google or Apple account you choose to link).

In the Game (instant)

  1. Open Piano Beats.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Under Support & legal, tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

This permanently erases your progress, coins, items and settings on the device, deletes your cloud backup from Firebase and deletes your Firebase account (anonymous or linked). Uninstalling the Game removes local data only.

If you already uninstalled the Game, email us (below) and we will delete your cloud backup and account within 30 days.

By email

For any other request, email appsmagic2942@gmail.com with the subject Piano Beats data request. We reply within 30 days.

Advertising data

Google AdMob keeps ad data under its own policies. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
