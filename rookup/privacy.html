Learn Chess: RookUp

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Learn Chess: RookUp ("the app") is published by Fluffy AI ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your learning progress stays on your device. Free users may choose to watch rewarded video ads, which are provided by Google AdMob. Purchases of RookUp Pro are handled by Apple or Google. We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that your progress is kept between sessions:

  • The name you type in (optional, any nickname).
  • Learning progress: strength number, practice stars, solved puzzles, completed lessons, win and loss counts and streaks.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration, move hints, auto-queen and the selected board style.
  • Daily usage on the free plan (hints and game reviews used today) and the date of your last daily puzzle.
  • Whether RookUp Pro is active on this device.

We do not receive this information and cannot see it. If your device backs up app data to your own cloud account (for example iCloud or Google backup), that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their terms.

Advertising (free plan only)

Free users can choose to watch a short rewarded video to get extra hints or game reviews. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one, and they are never shown to RookUp Pro users.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers (for example the advertising ID on Android, or an app-specific identifier on iOS),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about the ad and how you interact with it,
  • basic device, app and diagnostic information.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. The app does not ask iOS for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. Learn how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

RookUp Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase or subscription is active. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to the payment.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or browsing history. The app does not use the camera or microphone. The app contains no analytics software of our own.

Children

The app is a general-audience chess learning app and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with content limited to a general-audience (G) rating. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Your choices and deleting data

  • To delete your progress: open Settings and choose "Reset all progress", or uninstall the app.
  • To change ad consent (where offered): Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • To stop all ads: buy RookUp Pro, or simply do not tap "Watch a short video".
  • To manage or cancel a subscription: use your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

Security

Communication with Apple, Google and AdMob uses encrypted connections. Progress saved on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
