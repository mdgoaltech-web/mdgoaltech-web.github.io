School Bell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

School Bell is a school and family planner made by AFEX Digital. It helps parents and caregivers turn school notices into events, tasks, fee reminders and a "Ready for Tomorrow" checklist. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Everything you add to School Bell stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync in this version.
  • AFEX Digital does not receive, see or store your planner data.
  • School Bell has no ads and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Children: a first name or nickname, an optional school and class, a colour, an icon and school days and times. School Bell never asks for birth dates, photos of children, addresses, health information, identity documents or location.
  • Plans you create: events, tasks, timetables, fee reminders (amounts only if you type them), checklists and notes.
  • Notices you choose to import: pasted text, photos, screenshots or PDF files. They are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Names of other adults you add so you can assign tasks. These are labels only, not accounts.
  • Settings such as language, time zone, date format and reminder preferences.

Permissions

  • Photos, camera and files: used only when you choose to import a notice. School Bell opens the system picker or camera, and only the item you pick is copied into the app.
  • Notifications: used only if you turn reminders on. Lock-screen text is private by default, so child names and places are not shown.
  • School Bell cannot read WhatsApp, SMS, email or school portals, and it never opens links found inside notices.

Family Plus purchases

  • Family Plus is an optional subscription sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes the payment and we never see your payment details.
  • The store tells the app only whether Family Plus is active, so it can unlock the extra features and restore them on a new device.

Children

  • School Bell is meant for adults: parents and authorised caregivers. Children do not use the app and do not have accounts.
  • The small amount of information about children that a parent enters stays on the parent's device and is never used for advertising or profiling.

Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • In the app, More → Export and backup lets you export everything as a file or erase all data on the device. Deleting a child profile removes that child's records.
  • Uninstalling School Bell removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

Security

  • Data is kept in the app's private storage and protected by your device's own security. We recommend using a device passcode.

Changes

  • If a future version adds accounts, sync or automatic notice reading, we will update this policy before that version is released and explain in the app what is sent and why. Manual planning will always work without sending anything.
